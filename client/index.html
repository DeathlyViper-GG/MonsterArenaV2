❓ How to Play

Controls

W A S D / Arrows to move • Aim with mouse • Click shoot
R reload • Q/E switch weapon • Space dash • Esc pause • H help

Mobile: left stick to move • right round button to fire • WPN to switch.

What’s new

  • Enter buildings via doorways; some hide chests with ammo/powerups/health/shield.
  • Chests are hidden until you’re inside the building.
  • Enemies act in squads with roles (leader/flanker/support), share alerts, react to gunshots, and chase within a detection radius.
  • Later levels add lethal hazards (sand/chasm/lava/void) that kill everyone on touch.
